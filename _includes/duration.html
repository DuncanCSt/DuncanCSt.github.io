{%- comment -%} Renders how long a role lasted, e.g. "8 mos" or "1 yr 4 mos". Params: start "YYYY-MM" end "YYYY-MM", or "present" Counts inclusively — both the start and end months count — which is the convention LinkedIn and most resumes use (Aug 2022 to Apr 2024 reads as 1 yr 9 mos, not 1 yr 8 mos). An `end: present` role is measured against site.time, which is the moment the site was last built, so its duration only advances when the site rebuilds. Both separators are explicit non-breaking spaces rather than literal spaces: whitespace sitting between two adjacent Liquid tags is removed by the whitespace-control hyphens used throughout this file. They also stop "1 yr 9 mos" from wrapping inside the narrow date column. {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign _s = include.start | split: "-" -%} {%- assign _sy = _s[0] | times: 1 -%} {%- assign _sm = _s[1] | times: 1 -%} {%- if include.end == "present" -%} {%- assign _ey = site.time | date: "%Y" | times: 1 -%} {%- assign _em = site.time | date: "%m" | times: 1 -%} {%- else -%} {%- assign _e = include.end | split: "-" -%} {%- assign _ey = _e[0] | times: 1 -%} {%- assign _em = _e[1] | times: 1 -%} {%- endif -%} {%- assign _total = _ey | minus: _sy | times: 12 | plus: _em | minus: _sm | plus: 1 -%} {%- assign _yrs = _total | divided_by: 12 -%} {%- assign _mos = _total | modulo: 12 -%} {%- capture _out -%} {%- if _yrs > 0 -%} {{ _yrs }} {% if _yrs == 1 %}yr{% else %}yrs{% endif %} {%- endif -%} {%- if _mos > 0 -%} {%- if _yrs > 0 %} {% endif -%} {{ _mos }} {% if _mos == 1 %}mo{% else %}mos{% endif %} {%- endif -%} {%- endcapture -%} {{- _out | strip -}}
